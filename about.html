Questions players ask

The straight answers.

What does Sheetmancer do?

You take photos of your paper character (the character sheet plus the spell lists, charts, gear pages, backstory and notes that go with it) and Sheetmancer reads them into one editable Character Hub on your phone or tablet. Up to 10 pages per scan, typed or handwritten.

Does it work with my game?

Yes, if your character is on paper. Sheetmancer works with any tabletop RPG and any edition, including small-press games, older editions and homebrew, because it reads the character in front of it instead of forcing a template. Your game's own words stay as written: Hull Integrity stays Hull Integrity, Sanity stays Sanity.

Can it read handwriting?

Usually, yes. Typed, printed, filled-in PDF and handwritten pages all work, and a mix is fine. Clear handwriting reads well; very faint pencil or crowded margins are where mistakes come from, so glance over the result and fix a field or two, the same way you would proofread a photocopy.

How many pages can it scan?

Up to 10 pages of one character in a single Character Scan, so a full spellcaster with several pages of spells fits in one go. Found a stray page later? Add it to the character you already have.

How much does Sheetmancer cost?

Sheetmancer is $9.99, one time, with no ads and no subscription. That includes three Character Scans of up to 10 pages each. Extra scans are a one-time buy too, singly or in a five-pack.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Sheetmancer is on App Store & Google Play, for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Sign in with Apple or Google and your characters sync across your devices.

Do I need the internet?

Only to scan. Viewing and editing your saved characters, the Session Journal and the Dice Library all work offline.

Does it use AI? Will it change my character?

A machine-vision model reads the text on your photos and places it into editable fields. It only reads what is on your paper: it does not invent stats, rules, art or backstory, and every field is yours to edit afterwards.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, from inside the app at any time (how to delete). Deleting your account removes your characters and everything tied to it. The privacy policy explains exactly how your data is handled.

Can it scan the whole party at once?

No. One scan is one character, so each player scans their own.
