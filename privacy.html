Sheetmancer Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026
This is the app-specific privacy policy for Sheetmancer. It supplements the general DigiRune Studios Privacy Policy. Where the two address Sheetmancer differently, this page (the more specific one) governs.

Sheetmancer is a mobile app by DigiRune Studios, a division of McCullough Development, Inc. (a South Carolina corporation based in Beaufort, South Carolina, USA). The app lets you photograph or upload your tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG) character sheet and every page that goes with it (spells, gear, backstory, notes) and turn them into one editable digital character, a Character Hub, that syncs across your devices. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and how. We built Sheetmancer to respect your privacy: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

The short version. You can look around the app without an account. To scan your pages you sign in with your Apple or Google account; your account holds your Character Scans and syncs your characters across your devices. We store your characters securely in the cloud, tied to your account, and a copy stays on your device so the app works offline. Before your first scan the app tells you that an AI service reads your pages and asks for your OK. When you scan, the photos or PDFs are sent to our AI provider only to read the text on them. They are not used to train any AI and are deleted shortly after. We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and you can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we handle

Your account

To scan pages and sync your characters you sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive a unique account identifier from that provider (and, depending on the provider and your choices, a name and/or email address) so we can recognize you and link your characters to your account. We use a trusted authentication service, Google Firebase Authentication, to manage sign-in securely. We do not receive or store your Apple or Google password.

Your character data

The digital characters you create or edit (names, stats, notes, optional portrait images, themes, and session journal entries) are stored in two places: securely in the cloud using Google Firebase / Firestore, tied to your account so they sync across your devices, and as a local copy on each device so the app works offline. Your character data is private to your account; we do not browse, share, or sell it.

Portraits in larger accounts. When an account holds a lot of portrait pictures, each portrait is stored in your cloud account as its own picture, linked to your characters. When you replace a portrait or delete a character, the earlier portrait picture is not removed from the cloud right away: it stays in your account, private to you, until you delete your account.

Photo Album. Pictures you add to a character's Photo Album are stored in your cloud account (Google Firestore) so they appear on all your devices. They are deleted when you delete them, when you delete that character while signed in and online, or when you delete your account. If a character is deleted while the device is offline or signed out, its Photo Album pictures stay in your cloud account until you delete your account. When the app keeps two copies of one character (after a sync it could not settle on its own), the two copies either share one Photo Album (its pictures are deleted only when the last copy is deleted) or each keep their own.

Your settings. Your account also keeps a note that you accepted the in-app AI disclosure, so you are not asked again on every device.

Character photos and PDFs you scan

When you take or upload photos (or a PDF) of your character pages and create your character, those pages are transmitted over a secure connection to our processing service (see Section 2) so the text on the pages can be extracted into digital fields. We do not store your photos or PDFs on our servers. The finished result (text only, not the photos or PDFs) is kept on our scanning service for up to one hour, so a dropped connection can still collect it without paying twice, and is then deleted.

On your device, the pages you add are kept until the scan finishes or you cancel (for up to three days, so closing the app by accident does not lose them), and a finished scan result is kept until your character is safely saved (for up to seven days). Neither leaves your device.

Purchases

Sheetmancer is a paid app. Your purchase includes three Character Scans, and you can buy more scans (one at a time or a pack of five) as optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. DigiRune Studios never sees or stores your payment card details.

So that every purchase is credited exactly once, our scanning service keeps, linked to your account: a record of each purchase (the store's transaction ID, the product, the number of scans and the date), your Character Scan balance (scans included, bought and used), and a note that your Apple or Google sign-in has already received the scans included with the app. On Android, the app also asks Google Play to confirm it is a genuine copy; that check result is kept for about 15 minutes, and a short-lived counter tied to your network address (about an hour) limits how often that check can run.

To keep the service fair and running, our scanning service also keeps short-lived counters of how often scans are started on your account (they expire on their own after about a day).

Notes you send us (optional)

The app's Help has a Report a problem or suggest an idea link. If you choose to use it, we receive your message, the kind of note (problem, idea or other), the app name and version, your device type (iPhone, Android or web), the address of the page you sent it from and, only if you add it, your email address so we can reply. A random code kept on your device lets us limit how many notes one device can send in a day. Your network (IP) address is never stored with your note. Notes are kept for one year and then deleted automatically; to have one deleted sooner, email us.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • No location data, contacts, or microphone access.
  • Camera, photo and file access are used only when you choose to add a page, a portrait or a Photo Album picture.
  • We do not build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites.

2. Our AI processing service

To read the text on your character sheets, Sheetmancer sends the photos or PDFs to Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI models, through a secure server we operate. We use Anthropic's commercial API for this purpose: turning the writing on your page into editable fields (and, separately, AI may help sort through notes you choose to send us).

Per Anthropic's commercial API terms, inputs and outputs sent through the API are not used to train Anthropic's AI models, and they are automatically deleted from Anthropic's systems after a short retention period (currently about 7 days) kept only to detect misuse. Your character photos and PDFs are never used to build or improve any AI model. Anthropic's data practices are described at privacy.claude.com.

3. How your information is used

  • To sign you in and recognize your account across your devices.
  • To extract the text from your character sheet photos or PDFs and build your digital character.
  • To store and sync your characters so they appear on all your devices.
  • To process optional in-app purchases through the app stores, and to keep your Character Scan balance.
  • If you send us a note, to fix problems, improve the app and, if you included your email, reply to you. AI may help sort through the notes.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app's core features and answering notes you send us.

4. Service providers we rely on

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app, each for the limited purpose described:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore): to sign you in and store/sync your characters securely.
  • Anthropic: to read your scan photos or PDFs and return the extracted text, as described in Section 2; AI may also help sort through any notes you send us.
  • Cloudflare: the secure server that relays scan requests to Anthropic and keeps your Character Scan balance and purchase records; it also receives and stores any note you choose to send us, and, when you open the note form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a note comes from a person and not a spam robot.
  • Apple / Google: sign-in and purchase processing.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data security & retention

Sign-in and cloud storage are handled through Google Firebase's secure infrastructure, and scan photos and PDFs are transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We keep your account and character data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 6), your characters, Photo Album pictures, settings, scan balance and account record are removed from our cloud storage. We keep only what is needed to stop a purchase or the included scans from being credited twice (the purchase records and the note described in Section 1) and a small deletion record; these hold no character data.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Delete a character at any time from within the app; the change syncs to your other devices.
  • Delete your account and all your data from inside the app. This permanently removes your characters and account record from our cloud storage. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion here instead; no reinstall is needed.
  • Back up all your characters into one file at any time (Help menu or Account), or any one character from inside it.
  • Sign out on a device to remove your characters from that device. They stay in your account and come back when you sign in again.
  • Decline camera or photo access in your device settings at any time.

To request help with your data, contact us at the address in Section 9. Full instructions for deleting your account (in the app or by request) are on the Delete Your Account page.

7. Children's privacy

Sheetmancer is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your information, we will update this policy and revise the date above, and where appropriate notify you in the app, before the change takes effect.
